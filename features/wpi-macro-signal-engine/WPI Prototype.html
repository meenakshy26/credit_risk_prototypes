How to read the flags

The golden rule: if the thing a borrower BUYS is going up faster than the thing they SELL, the borrower is earning less.

Each price is compared with 3 months ago: moved more than 10% either way → ORANGE — look into it; more than 20% → RED — act.

This tool reads market prices only. It does not know the borrower's stock quantity, purchase price or leverage, it does not watch the news (it tells you which news to check), and it is not a credit decision — it is an early-warning instrument for you to test.
